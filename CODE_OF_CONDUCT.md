# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short version of it.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the work, not the person. Welcome people of every background and experience level, give and accept feedback graciously, and assume good faith.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, use the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability** to reach the maintainer privately, and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence.
